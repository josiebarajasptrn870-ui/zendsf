Cookies Policy

We use cookies to make your visit as smooth as a forest breeze. This policy explains what they are, how we use them, and how you can manage them.

📅 last updated: 30 June 2026 🍪 version 1.4

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and keep the site running securely. At Zenthra Nature Canvas, we use only necessary and analytical cookies — never intrusive or advertising-based.

Cookies we use

Below is a transparent list of all cookies active on our site. We keep it simple and minimal.

Name Type Purpose Duration
zenthra_session Essential Maintains your session and preferences (e.g., dark mode, font size) Session
zenthra_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent choice 1 year
_ga (anonymized) Analytical Privacy-friendly analytics to understand page visits (no personal data) 2 years
zenthra_pref Functional Remembers your preferred language and region 6 months

How we use cookies

🔐 Essential
Site security, session management, and consent storage.
📈 Analytical
We use a privacy-first analytics tool (no cross-site tracking) to see which articles resonate most.
⚙️ Functional
Remember your preferences (language, layout) so you don’t have to set them each time.

Third-party cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or social media trackers. Our analytics are self-hosted and anonymized. We believe in a quiet, respectful web — no data brokers, no hidden surveillance.

Manage your cookies

You can control or delete cookies at any time. Most browsers allow you to block or remove cookies via their settings. However, please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our site.

🌿 Quick links: Chrome · Firefox · Safari · Edge

Your consent

By using our website, you agree to the placement of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or adjusting your browser settings. We also provide a cookie preference banner on your first visit.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. We’ll always post the latest version here with the “last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to check back occasionally.
