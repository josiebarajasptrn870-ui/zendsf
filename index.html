    Frequently asked questions

    What is no‑dig gardening and why does it matter?

    No‑dig preserves soil structure, reduces weed emergence, and fosters mycorrhizal networks. It’s a low‑disturbance approach that builds organic matter from the top down.

    How do I choose between heirloom and hybrid seeds?

    Heirlooms offer genetic diversity, flavour, and seed‑saving potential; hybrids often provide disease resistance. We recommend a mix tailored to your goals.

    What are the first steps in creating a food forest?

    Start with canopy trees, then understory, shrubs, herbaceous layers, and groundcovers. Focus on nitrogen‑fixers and dynamic accumulators for fertility.
