Privacy Policy

Your data is safe with us. We believe in transparency, simplicity, and respect for your digital footprint — just as we care for the soil.

📅 last updated: 30 June 2026 🌿 version 2.1

Our commitment

At Zenthra Nature Canvas, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us. We never sell your data — and we only use it to make your experience better.

What we collect

🌱 Information you give us

  • Name and email address (via forms, newsletter, or comments)
  • Messages you send through our contact form
  • Any feedback or survey responses

🌿 Information we collect automatically

  • IP address and browser type (for analytics)
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referring website or search engine

How we use your information

📬 respond to inquiries
We reply to your messages and questions.
📨 send occasional updates
Newsletter, garden tips, and seasonal offers.
📊 improve our content
Analytics help us understand what you enjoy most.
🔒 protect our site
Monitor for fraud, spam, or security issues.

Cookies & third parties

We use essential cookies to keep the site running smoothly, and optional analytics cookies (via a privacy-friendly service) to understand how our content is used. We do not use tracking ads or share data with third-party marketing platforms. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time via our Cookies Policy.

Your rights

🟢 Access
Request a copy of your data.
🟢 Rectify
Update or correct your information.
🟢 Erase
Ask us to delete your data (right to be forgotten).
🟢 Restrict
Limit how we use your data.

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us — we’ll respond within 30 days.

Data retention & security

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss. Our garden may be open, but our servers are locked.

Children’s privacy

Zenthra Nature Canvas does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We’ll post the updated version here and indicate the “last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review it periodically.

📬 Privacy questions? — Reach our data protection lead at privacy@zenthra.nature or via our contact page.
